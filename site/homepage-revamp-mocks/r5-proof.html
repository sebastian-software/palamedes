01 — Benchmarks

Three corpora, two lanes, one rule: every number re-runs.

The comparison lane runs cold — every cache cleared, every tool doing the same extract-and-update job. The warm lane is Palamedes against itself, because no compared tool has a per-file cache to race.

Exact boundary
