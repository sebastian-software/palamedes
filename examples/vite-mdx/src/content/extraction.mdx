# Extract once, render everywhere

Palamedes turns rendered semantics into stable translation units:

- headings and paragraphs become focused messages
- **rich text** keeps its structure as placeholders
- expressions keep readable names
- image descriptions are extracted separately

![Three stages connected from MDX source to catalog and runtime](/pipeline.svg)
